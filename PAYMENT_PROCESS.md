Manual purchase / interim flow (placeholder)

Until you have real payment integrations (Gumroad, Stripe, PayPal), use this interim manual flow to accept paid requests.

1) Buyer opens an issue using the Paid feature request template.
2) Issue gets labeled `paid-feature` (maintainer or automation). An automated comment will ask for budget and contact details.
3) Maintainer replies with a quote and a payment request. Use PayPal.me, bank transfer, or send an invoice via Stripe/Braintree.
4) Once payment is received, mark the issue with `bounty` and begin work. Upload deliverables to a release or attach to the issue.

This lets you accept paid work immediately without external storefronts.
