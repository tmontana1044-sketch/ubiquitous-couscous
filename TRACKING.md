Tracking & UTM guide

To measure which channels drive sponsors or sales, use UTM parameters on links you publish.

Example UTM structure for Twitter:
https://github.com/tmontana1044-sketch/ubiquitous-couscous?utm_source=twitter&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=announce_2026

Example for Gumroad product link (replace PRODUCT_SLUG):
https://gumroad.com/l/PRODUCT_SLUG?utm_source=github_readme&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=launch

Shortlink & analytics

- Create a Bitly or Rebrandly shortlink for each channel so you can see click counts at a glance.
- Alternatively, create a simple landing page under your control that forwards to the payment link and captures click logs.

Report cadence

- Check clicks and conversions daily for the first week, then weekly.
- Track: clicks, paid-feature issue opens, sponsor signups, purchases.
